# Population audit — 2026-09-07

All 32 catalog entries were reviewed against the public sources below. This is a dated evidence review, not a claim that every number is a current-year estimate. Sources have different publication lags and geographic definitions. No extrapolation or averaging was used.

The old headline drew on Wikidata or the ACS override without showing dates. The independently maintained city-population.json now controls headline population in cards and detail pages, survives data syncs, and records kind, date, boundary, source and caveats. Older snapshots remain unchanged for historical indicators: replacing their denominators with a newer population would mix years and definitions.

London: the previous 8,799,728 is the 2021 count. The GLA overview reports 9.1 million with a 2024 ONS reference. Store its published precision, display ≈9.1M, and do not invent an exact count from a rounded headline.

Census values retained pending a newer verified same-boundary observation: Vancouver, Mexico City, Dublin, Montréal, Ottawa, Edmonton and Halifax. Paris retains the 2023 official reference population published in 2026. Montréal's previous 1,895,211 was located in a 2023 financial report but its reference date could not be established; the verified 2021 municipal census count replaces that undated claim. Halifax's census source includes a use-with-caution flag.

Berlin: the annual landing page's table and prose disagree. The specific dated 2025 press release states 3,700,577 and is the selected source; this is different from registered population. Hamburg likewise uses Census 2022-based estimates. Munich explicitly uses the main-residence register.

## Maintenance

Update src/data/city-population.json only after checking the reference date, geographic scope and population definition on the linked publisher page. A source checked in 2026 may describe a 2021 census. Do not substitute projections or commuter-region totals. Tests require a reviewed observation for every catalog city and verify that displayed population and source metadata use the reviewed observation.
